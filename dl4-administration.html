Retainer scope

Included categories, not an hour pool.

Capacity is defined from day one as scope categories plus a 2 hour response SLA. Generous by design. Work outside these categories is scoped as project work.

  • Configuration and rule changesDecision engine updates made deliberately, tested, and documented rather than queued behind everything else IT owns.
  • Compliance updatesIncluding High Priced Mortgage Loan rules on home equity products, implemented in the engine rather than tracked on a spreadsheet.
  • Vendor coordinationWorking with MeridianLink and adjacent vendors so tickets, releases, and configuration questions do not stall lending.
  • Operational administrationDay to day DL4 ownership in place of a dedicated LOS administrator, or alongside internal staff who already have a full plate.
  • Advisory within the engagementGuidance on decisioning design and change impact, inside the retainer rather than billed as a separate opinion.

Outside retainer scope

Net-new product builds, major migrations, and multi-week initiatives are scoped as project work at $225 to $275 per hour.
