How engagements are staffed

Senior coverage, with no bench to subsidise.

Every hour is a senior hour

Larger firms win the engagement with their principals and deliver it with whoever is available. Here the person who scopes the work is the person who does it. There is no junior staff learning your environment on your retainer, and nothing is handed down a tier.

Capacity scales with the book

The practice adds vetted contract capacity as engagement volume requires, under the same scope categories and the same response SLA. That keeps the model honest: capacity is defined in the agreement rather than promised against a headcount you cannot see.

Continuity is a deliberate part of the offer. A single accountable point of contact is the reason work does not stall in a queue, and it is what a pooled vendor support team cannot match.
